About 4Dev Studio

A small studio building
software that actually fits.

4Dev Studio is a small, senior team of engineers and designers. We build custom web apps, mobile apps, and business systems for companies that are tired of bending their workflow to fit the tool.

Our story

Why "4Dev"?

The name comes from a simple idea: four ways we help businesses move faster — automate operations, automate sales, automate the business, and build new ideas from scratch. Every project we take on is some combination of those four.

We started 4Dev Studio because we kept seeing the same pattern: companies trying to run serious operations on tools that were never designed for them. Spreadsheets doing the work of databases. Group chats doing the work of workflows. Off-the-shelf software forcing teams to reshape how they work just to fit inside it.

We build the opposite. Software shaped around how a business actually runs — not how a template assumes it should.

How we work

Four principles we don't bend on.

01

Outcomes, not hours

We quote fixed scope and fixed price. You know what you're getting and what it costs before we start. No hourly billing, no scope creep, no surprise invoices at the end.

02

You see progress every week

Working demos every Friday. Real, clickable software from week one — not status reports or slide decks. If something's off track, you'll know long before launch.

03

You own everything

Full source access, documentation, and hosting guidance. If you ever want to take it in-house or hand it to another team, you can. No lock-in, ever.

04

Senior people only

Every person on your project has shipped production software before. No juniors learning on your budget, no handoffs to a second team, no surprise turnover mid-project.
